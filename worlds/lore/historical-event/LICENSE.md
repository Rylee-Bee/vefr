## Sources this pack draws on

This pack is a *derivative work*, of - but its *textures* are the
lore pack's own original prose. The pack does not quote historical
sources at length. The pack's *vocabulary* is shaped by the kind of
language that primary-source historical writing tends to use: name,
place, witness, the part the record left out. The pack deliberately
keeps that vocabulary at arm's length.

For the *real* research that uses this pack as a mood-board,
please source primary historical documents directly:

- **Public-domain archives** - Project Gutenberg, Internet Archive,
  government archives, university libraries. These have well-tested
  rules for what is and isn't in the public domain.
- **Public-domain history books** - especially those published
  before 1929 in the United States. Many landmark works of the
  19th and early 20th centuries are public domain.
- **First-person accounts** - letters, oral histories, memoirs.
  These have their own publication and copyright concerns; check
  with an archivist or copyright lawyer if you're unsure.

## Real names and this pack

This pack's `names.md` deliberately avoids real people's names.
Names in this pack are **role descriptors** ("the bearer," "the
looker-on," "the one who came back"). When you build a world
using this pack, please continue this convention unless you have
explicit permission from the people you're naming.

If you are researching your own family history or community
history, you may use real names in your own world - that's your
family, your community, your right. This pack's convention is
*for the default*, when you don't have that explicit relationship.

## What this pack is NOT

This pack does **not** contain:
- Real names of real people who lived through real events.
- Quotations from historical primary sources.
- A claim that any specific historical event is more or less
  important than any other.
- Religious or political content from any specific denomination,
  party, or nation.

The pack is a *literary mood-board*. Worlds built from it are
fiction. The pack is for fiction, not for historical work.
