# Norse - word-hoard (suggested names)

Names in the Norse flavor carry weight. They're often compound:
- a kennings (whale-road = sea, sword-hunger = battle)
- a place name (Hliðskjálf, Ásgarðr, Miðgarðr)
- a personal epithet (Fair-haired, Loud-laughter, the Skald of X)

A handful to seed the world's word-hoard. The author can pick
which to keep, change what they like, or invent more.

## Travelers (skalds, smiths, and wanderers)
- **Bragi** - the one who composes; speaks first, always
- **Lofn** - the one who opens doors; praise-poetry is her work
- **Idunn** - the one who keeps; her apples renew what's tired
- **Dagny** - new-day; carries the renewal Lofn brought
- **Sigyn** - the one who stays; bowls emptied are her fidelity
- **Kvasir** - the mead-brewer whose wisdom came from many

## Places
- **Hliðskjálf** - the high seat; vantage from which one sees all
- **Ásgarðr** - the walled place; where the powers keep their things
- **Miðgarðr** - the middle enclosure; where mortals live
- **Útgarðr** - the outer; what lies beyond the fences
- **Niflheim** - the mist-ward; cold and dark, where the first
  cold came from
- **Bifröst** - the trembling path; the bridge between here and
  elsewhere

## Things & attributes
- **whale-road** - the sea (so travelers say it; sailors say "sea")
- **sword-hunger** - the appetite for battle
- **word-hoard** - the store of verses a skald keeps
- **bone-locked** - oath-bound, beyond recall
- **fire-hearted** - one who carries the kind of anger that warms
- **cold-runes** - written things that lose their warmth
- **bright-sung** - something the town still believes in

## Epithets for the protagonist
- **the wrong-named** - if the world miscalls her
- **the bright-sung** - if she's earned a true name
- **the bend-and-weave** - if she's the wanderer
- **the warm-handed** - the way the keeper's hand lingers on the
  latch

The list is a seed, not a catalog. Add what the world asks for.
