# Norse - wandering questions

These are the questions the world's mouth opens to ask. Each one
is what the skald *wants* to know - the gap they keep returning to.
The author uses them to figure out what's not yet said.

Some of these are answered by `norns craft chat`. Some are
answered by the author in private. Some are never answered, and
that is fine - the world keeps asking.

## About the place
1. **Where does the road come from, and where does it go?**
   Every Norse town is on a road. The road is older than the town,
   older than the kingdom. What's at its ends?

2. **What did the bell toll for, the first time?**
   Norse bells toll for one thing at a time. They are not
   general-purpose. What was the first thing this bell rang for,
   and is it still ringing for it?

3. **Who is the keeper of this road?**
   Not the warden - the keeper. The one who keeps the names of
   everyone who has walked it, in some order that matters to no
   one but them.

## About the people
1. **What name did the town give the protagonist, and why that name?**
   The wrong name is the most Norse thing in the world. Names are
   how the world decides if you belong.

2. **Whose song is the town singing about right now?**
   Not the town's song - the *one the town can't stop singing*.
   The one it hums when it doesn't know it's humming.

3. **What did the keeper of the bell bring to the bell today, and
   what did they leave with?**
   The keeper trades. The bell keeps the trade. The thing left
   is usually a small object that won't be missed for a day.

## About the things that happen
1. **What word did the protagonist almost say, and why didn't she?**
   The word she almost said is the word that would have changed
   the phase. The not-saying is the phase.

2. **When was the last time the bell rang twice in one day?**
   Twice-in-one-day is a Norse catastrophe. Whatever happened that
   day is the town's oldest buried thing.

3. **What's the smallest thing the town has forgotten?**
   Bigger forgets are mentioned in the logbok. The smallest is
   what makes the protagonist ask a question she doesn't know
   she's asking.

## About the ending
1. **Who writes the protagonist's song when she's done?**
   Norse skalds don't write their own songs. Someone else writes
   theirs. Who is going to write hers?

2. **What does the bell toll for, the last time?**
   The first tolling is the question. The last is the answer.
   The world is the time between.
