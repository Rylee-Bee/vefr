# Norse runes - wandering questions

The questions a rune-cast-flavored world asks. They are the
questions the cast asks after the chips land, the questions the
protagonist asks when she doesn't know what the world is asking
yet.

## About the cast
1. **What three runes did you throw, and what position did they
   land in?** Three runes is a small cast - enough to see the
   situation, not so many it becomes a reading. The position is
   what part of the situation the rune is pointing at.

2. **What rune is missing from the cast?** The runes not in the
   cast are the ones that didn't surface. The town doesn't know
   about them. The protagonist doesn't know what she's missing.

3. **What rune would you put back in the bag, and why?** The
   runes you don't want to see are the ones the world is most
   likely to need.

## About the protagonist
1. **What rune is she?** Not the one she has. The one she *is*.
   The Mannaz to her Hagalaz, the Isa to her Sowilo. The rune
   she carries is the one the world reads when it looks at her.

2. **What rune does she keep turning over in her hand?** The
   rune she doesn't know yet. The rune the cast keeps giving her
   even when she asks the question differently. The rune is
   waiting.

3. **What rune did she almost carve, and why didn't she?** The
   rune she wanted to add is the rune she wasn't ready for. The
   not-carving is the rune's protection of her.

## About the place
1. **What rune is carved on the threshold, and what rune has
   worn off?** The threshold has a rune. It used to have another.
   The worn-off rune is the question the threshold stopped
   asking.

2. **What rune is in the well, and who put it there?** The well
   has a rune. The rune is older than the town. The rune was put
   there by someone who knew what the water was.

3. **What rune would you carve on the bell, and what rune would
   you carve on the door?** The bell and the door are the two
   openings. Each has its own rune. The runes you choose say
   what kind of town this is.

## About the ending
1. **What rune do you keep, and what rune do you give away?**
   The rune you keep is the one that became you. The rune you
   give away is the one that needs someone else.

2. **What rune would you carve on the protagonist's stone, when
   her time is done?** The rune on a stone is the rune the town
   remembers. The author decides. The rune is the last cast.
