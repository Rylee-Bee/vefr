# Norse runes - cards (rune-by-rune mapping)

Each rune has a name, a stave (shape), a rune poem (in names.md),
and an **engine interpretation** - the engine's own gloss that
ties the rune to a phase of the Hero's Journey and a moment the
world might ask the player to come back to.

The Hero's Journey stages and the engine's phase names align:

| Journey stage | Engine phase |
|---|---|
| Ordinary world | (before play) |
| Call to adventure | whispers |
| Refusal / threshold | doubts |
| Tests / allies / enemies | feared |
| Revelation / return | awed |

## First aettir (the upper / the call)

### Fehu (wealth, the seed-fire)
- **Phase:** whispers
- **Moment:** the player's first gift or first loss
- **Reading:** "Wealth is a comfort to all men." The bell's first
  tolling is Fehu - the town has the bell, the bell is the town's,
  and the player is the one who carries the wealth now.

### Uruz (the aurochs)
- **Phase:** whispers
- **Moment:** strength that doesn't know it's strength yet
- **Reading:** "The aurochs is fierce and gory, horned and
  hide-bound." The protagonist is the aurochs - slow, wild, not
  yet grown to what she will be.

### Thurisaz (the thorn)
- **Phase:** doubts
- **Moment:** the first refusal, the first "no" the world says
- **Reading:** "The thorn is sharp and severe, an evil thing for
  any knight to touch." The threshold has a thorn in it. The
  player must touch it to pass.

### Ansuz (the word)
- **Phase:** doubts
- **Moment:** a message arrives
- **Reading:** "The mouth is the source of all language." The
  witness speaks; the keeper listens; the bell, when it rings,
  is Ansuz speaking through the air.

### Raido (the ride, the road)
- **Phase:** fears → feared
- **Moment:** the player begins to move
- **Reading:** "Riding is easy for the warrior who sits in the
  stirrups." The protagonist's first *going* is Raido - the road
  takes her, and the road is the rune.

### Kenaz (the torch)
- **Phase:** feared
- **Moment:** something is revealed
- **Reading:** "The torch is known by its burning." The witness
  becomes the flame - what is known by the reading is known by
  burning.

### Gebo (the gift)
- **Phase:** feared
- **Moment:** an exchange
- **Reading:** "Generosity brings credit and honour." The keeper
  gives; the player receives; both are changed.

### Wunjo (joy, the wind-smooth stone)
- **Phase:** feared → awed
- **Moment:** the moment of unexpected welcome
- **Reading:** "Joy is a comfort to those who know it." The
  wind-smooth stone is what the runes feel like after the cast -
  smooth, patient, worn.

## Second aettir (the middle / the tests)

### Hagalaz (hail, the seed-grain)
- **Phase:** fears, the breaking
- **Moment:** the event that breaks the cycle
- **Reading:** "Hail is the coldest of grains; it whirls down
  from the heavens." The hail is what comes down when the bell
  rings the wrong way.

### Nauthiz (need)
- **Phase:** fears, the constraint
- **Moment:** the wall
- **Reading:** "Trouble is oppressive to the heart; yet often
  it becomes a help." The wall is also the door.

### Isa (ice)
- **Phase:** the stillness between fears and awed
- **Moment:** the held breath
- **Reading:** "Ice is the bark of rivers." The river of the
  journey freezes for a moment. The river is not gone.

### Jera (the year, the harvest)
- **Phase:** awed
- **Moment:** the return
- **Reading:** "The harvest is a joy to men." What was sown is
  reaped. The harvest is the awed phase's gift.

### Eihwaz (the yew, the axis)
- **Phase:** the endurance
- **Moment:** the long walk
- **Reading:** "The yew is an unfriendly tree, hard to bend and
  brittle by nature, a guardian of flame." The protagonist is
  the yew. The yew is the protagonist. Both stand.

### Perthro (the dice-cup)
- **Phase:** the hidden
- **Moment:** the lot is cast
- **Reading:** "The dice-cup is the luck of the play." The cast
  is a Perthro. Whatever lands, that is the cast.

### Algiz (the elk-sedge, the sanctuary)
- **Phase:** the hearth
- **Moment:** the safe place
- **Reading:** "The elk-sedge grows in the marsh, a
  wound-stancher." The hearth is Algiz. The room that stays shut
  is Algiz.

### Sowilo (the sun)
- **Phase:** awed, the gold
- **Moment:** the moment of light
- **Reading:** "The sun is ever the traveller's friend." The
  gold-light, the awed tone, the brightness the player earned by
  coming back - Sowilo.

## Third aettir (the lower / the return)

### Tiwaz (the star, the right hand)
- **Phase:** the vow
- **Moment:** the moment the protagonist gives her word
- **Reading:** "Tyr is a star, the keeper of the sky." The word
  the protagonist gives is Tiwaz. The word cannot be taken back.

### Berkano (the birch, the birth)
- **Phase:** the second birth
- **Moment:** the new self
- **Reading:** "The birch is the leafiest of trees, with branches
  that bear the burden of fruit." The protagonist after the
  journey is the birch - the same tree, leafier.

### Ehwaz (the horse)
- **Phase:** the partnership
- **Moment:** the one who walks with her
- **Reading:** "The horse is the joy of the warrior, the wealth
  of the wanderer, the partner of the lord." the ferryman. Old
  the soldier. Whoever walks with the protagonist is Ehwaz.

### Mannaz (the human, the self)
- **Phase:** every phase
- **Moment:** the protagonist herself
- **Reading:** "The human is the delight of the home, the
  comfort of the kin." The protagonist is Mannaz. The rune is
  the protagonist.

### Laguz (the water)
- **Phase:** the crossing, the awed
- **Moment:** the water that lets her pass
- **Reading:** "Water is the river's blood, the lake's mirror."
  The water is Laguz. The crossing is Laguz. The gold is in the
  water.

### Ingwaz (the seed)
- **Phase:** the inward, the gestation
- **Moment:** the time before the journey begins
- **Reading:** "The seed is the promise of the field." The seed
  is what's held inside before the journey. The seed is the
  moment of beginning.

### Othala (the heritage)
- **Phase:** the return home
- **Moment:** the home that was changed by being left
- **Reading:** "The heritage is the home of the home." The
  protagonist returns to a home that is the same and not the
  same. The home is Othala.

### Dagaz (the day)
- **Phase:** the ending, the dawn
- **Moment:** the morning that follows
- **Reading:** "The day is the dawn's own, the brightener of
  mornings." The day after the journey is Dagaz. The morning
  the protagonist walks into is Dagaz.
