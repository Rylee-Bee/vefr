# Scenario cards: help writing the sentences

Status: **working draft** for the next named-edits experiment (`design/named-edits.md`).

**How to use these.** Read a card. Imagine you are that person, in that moment, talking to a helper in a game studio.
Write what you would *actually say*, in one or two sentences, the way you'd say it out loud. Vague is good. Wrong is good.
Do not think about what the studio can do; that is what we are testing. Two sentences per card is plenty, so 20 cards give 40.
Skip any card that doesn't spark anything. The games used are **Cottage of the Breeze** (a small painted cottage-and-town game) unless a card says blank.

The people on the cards are made up. They are here so the sentences come out different from each other.

**The people**
- **Maya**, a retired teacher. Careful, polite, afraid of breaking something. Reads every word.
- **Theo**, ten. Fast, funny, wants monsters and secrets, never reads instructions.
- **Iris**, a lore lover. Wants the world to make sense and to have a history.
- **Dev**, who hates typing. Dictates short sentences, drops words, says "that one" and "over there".

## A. The first ten minutes (blank world)

**A1. Maya opens the studio.** The screen offers "Begin a new world". Maya doesn't know what a world file is. They want to make a place for their grandchildren to wander in.
*What do they say first?*

**A2. Theo has an idea in the shower.** A game about a cat that runs a bakery at night. Theo opens the studio in a hurry before they forget.
*Say the idea the way a ten-year-old would, in one breath.*

**A3. Iris has a map in their head.** A harbour town built on the back of something huge and asleep. They want the town to *feel* old.
*What do they ask for to start?*

**A4. Dev stands at the screen with a headset.** The studio shows an empty room. Dev wants it to feel like a cosy library.
*Say it the way Dev would: short, pointing, no typing.*

## B. Making a person

**B1. Maya wants a kind neighbour.** Someone who waves from a garden gate and always has tea. Maya describes people by how they make you feel, not by what they look like.
*How do they ask for this person?*

**B2. Theo wants a grumpy guard.** The guard should be scary at first, then turn out to be a big softie who loves his pet frog.
*What does Theo say?*

**B3. Iris wants a historian who knows too much.** An old woman who remembers the town before the harbour. She says things that don't quite fit the official story.
*How does Iris describe her, her voice, and where she sits?*

**B4. Dev wants "the keeper" to sound warmer.** Dev tried the keeper once and thought they sounded like a form letter.
*What does Dev say, in the fewest words?*

## C. Fixing something that feels wrong

**C1. Maya played the cottage and it felt too empty.** There is a rug and a floor and not much else. Maya didn't want to say anything unkind about a game they made.
*What do they say about it?*

**C2. Theo walked into a monster and nothing exciting happened.** Theo expected a fight with a big noise.
*What does Theo complain about?*

**C3. Iris noticed the town has the same grass everywhere.** It looks fake to them, like wallpaper. They know there are "variants" but not what that means.
*How do they say it?*

**C4. Dev got lost.** Dev walked out of the cottage and couldn't tell which way the tavern was. Dev doesn't want a map; they want to be *told*.
*What does Dev ask for?*

## D. Adding a surprise

**D1. Maya wants a hidden thing.** A small door behind a bookshelf that opens onto a tiny garden. Maya thinks it would make their grandchildren gasp.
*How do they ask?*

**D2. Theo wants a trap that is also a joke.** The chest should look like treasure but be full of something silly. Theo's best friend will fall for it.
*What does Theo say?*

**D3. Iris wants weather with meaning.** The fog should roll in only when someone says the old name of the harbour.
*How does Iris put this?*

**D4. Dev wants the game to react to them.** When Dev walks past the cat, something small should happen, a sound, a look, a purr.
*What does Dev say?*

## E. Making an item

**E1. Maya wants a keepsake.** A teacup that was their mother's. It should be a thing a player can find and keep, and it should mean something.
*How do they describe it?*

**E2. Theo wants a super sword that is actually a spoon.** Theo wants everyone to take it seriously.
*What do they say?*

**E3. Iris wants a map fragment.** A torn piece of an old chart, one of several, that only makes sense when all the pieces are together.
*How does Iris ask?*

**E4. Dev wants "the key".** Dev says "that one, the old one, the one that opens the thing". The thing is a door that does not exist yet.
*Say what Dev says, including the vague bits.*

## Recording your sentences

For each card, write the sentence(s) in `design/named-edits-sentences.md` as:

```
A1: "..."
A1: "..."
```

Mark a card `skip` if nothing came. The next run maps each sentence to a named edit, applies the ones that fit to a throwaway copy,
and shows you the before and after as pictures. A person other than the author of the edits decides, for each result,
whether it feels like theirs.
