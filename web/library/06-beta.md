The final art is in. Fix bugs, smooth rough edges, tune how hard it is.

Let a friend play while you watch quietly. Wherever they get stuck is
your to-do list.

* * *

Check every picture, sound and font you used, and write down its
license. Watch for **scope creep**: the game quietly growing bigger.

**The gate:** a new player can finish it without your help.
