---
title: The Seven Stages
kind: book
---
Every game, from a weekend toy to a big studio's epic, moves through the
same seven stages. Each stage answers one question before the next begins.

The order is the point. Changing your mind is cheap on paper and
expensive in code, so studios spend real care on the first two stages.

* * *

1. **Concept.** Is this worth making?
2. **Pre-production.** Is the core fun, and can we build it?
3. **Production.** Build all of it.
4. **Alpha.** Can you play it start to finish?
5. **Beta.** Is it polished and solid?
6. **Launch.** Ship it to players.
7. **After launch.** What did we learn?

* * *

Every stage ends at a **gate**: a check you pass before moving on.
Studios call the first one a greenlight.

Gates are how you avoid building a whole game on an idea that isn't fun.
The books on this shelf take the stages one at a time.
