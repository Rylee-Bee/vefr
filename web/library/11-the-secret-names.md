The studio uses cosy words. Real studios and programmers have their own
words for the same things. Learn both, and you can talk to anyone.

* * *

**Keep:** saving something for good. Programmers say *commit*.

**The Chronicle:** the list of everything that happened, in order. Real
names: a *log*, or *version history*.

**Undo:** going back one step. In version history it's a *revert*.

**Weave (Make a shareable file):** turning your world into one finished
file. Programmers say *build*; sharing it is a *release*.

* * *

**A map's symbols:** each square of a map is really a letter in a text
file, like `#` for a wall. That's *data*, and the rules for reading it
are a *format*.

**Check the world:** a *test*, or *validation*. When it finds a problem,
fixing it is *debugging*.

**The slider (Show me how things work):** peeking at the *source*, the
real files underneath the pictures.

* * *

**A helper:** a small *AI model* (or a plain *program*) with one job.

**A template:** a *prompt*, the instructions a model reads first.

**A cup:** a *benchmark*, a test that measures helpers the same way.

**The Casting Table's runes:** *random generation*. Making new things
from rules and dice is called *procedural generation*.

* * *

**The rooms and their real jobs:** the Map Room is *level design*. The
Folks are *character design*. The Vault is the *item database*. The
Archives are the *source of truth*. The Boiler Room is *settings* and
*operations*. The Library is *documentation*, which is this book!
