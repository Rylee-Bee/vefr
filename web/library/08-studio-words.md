---
**Core loop:** the thing the player does over and over. If it isn't fun,
nothing else saves the game.

**Greenlight:** the yes that moves a game past concept.

**Design doc:** how the game works, written down.

* * *

**Greybox:** a level built from plain boxes, to test the layout before
any art.

**Prototype:** an ugly, throwaway version that answers one question.

**Vertical slice:** one small piece at final quality.

* * *

**Milestone:** a checkpoint with a playable build.

**Scope creep:** the game quietly growing bigger. The number one reason
games never ship.

**Placeholder:** stand-in art or words, to be replaced.

**Postmortem:** a short, honest look back after launch.
