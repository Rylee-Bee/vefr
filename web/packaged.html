      How to play

      • Move with the arrow keys or W A S D, or the pad on a touch screen.
      • Use what is near you with E, Space, Enter, F or the big Interact button. It always says what it will do. The same keys turn the pages of a note and close someone's speech, and E or F close a shop.
      • Hear a rumor with Q or the Whisper button.
      • Open your bag with B or the Bag button.
      • Walk the unexplored dark with O or the Explore button.
      • Turn the dark off with V, or in Display. Dusk and Dawn are in Display too, under Time of day.
      • Open and close this menu with Esc.

      Model settings

      This game can use your own language model for fresh lines. It plays fully without one.

      Display

      The dark shows only the ground you have walked and lit. Turn it off to see the whole map.

      Time of day

      The world's mood. What people say changes with it.

      Start over

      Start over? This clears your bag, gold, health, fog and the monsters you've beaten in this browser.
