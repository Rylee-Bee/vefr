Before you play

Play offline, or bring a model

This game plays fine on its own. If you run a language model, it can write the characters' lines fresh each time.

Works with llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio, or a phone running a local model server. Leave both blank to play offline; the game's own lines carry the world. Nothing leaves your device.
