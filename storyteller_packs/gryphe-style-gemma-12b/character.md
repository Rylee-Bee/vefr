# Character framing template (Tier 2/3 future use)

When a Tier 2 or Tier 3 storyteller is configured, the harness MAY
prepend a character-framing block to the system role that encodes:

- the speaker's name and physical state
- the speaker's relationship to the player
- the speaker's voice constraints (sentence length, dialect, etc.)
- any first-person scene directives the pack author wants enforced

Tier 1 packs (text only) do not need this. The pack author is free
to leave this file empty or omit it entirely.
