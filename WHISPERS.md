# The Ledger - collected whispers

Whispers Rylee has kept. The engine reads this ledger and matches
their cadence. Add freely; the voice compounds.

## whispers

- **Old Tamsin**: "Saw that boy from the bookshop at the well, mind
  you—no, not a boy at all, just hands like no one else's, and a look
  as if she were listening to something we can't hear."

- **Old Hrafn**: "They say she dragged a bell from the bog and it rang
  without a tongue, but I swear it was the river that spoke her name
  wrong."

## awed

- **Hilda**: "When the first frost silvered the church roof, the wanderer's
  ink froze in the well before the water did."

- **the ferryman**: "I saw the wanderer tie the parish ledger to the
  door-handle with her own ribbon, and every page that had been
  missing walked back into the room by morning."
