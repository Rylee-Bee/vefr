The drawer slides open with a whisper of hinges. Cold metal lies folded in the depths—a small iron key, no larger than a coin. It gleams dull in the lamplight, as if it has been waiting. Whatever it unlocks, the moment you touch it, you know, is the moment the study’s secrets shift.

---

The chest creaks open like an old door. The iron key twists smooth in its groove, but nothing moves inside. Dust swirls from the corners, and the air smells of polish and something faintly metallic. The moment is quiet—too quiet—and the space around you feels hollow.

---

The drawer stands hollow now, the key cold in your grip. The desk holds nothing but dust and the faint scent of old parchment, the same as before you opened it. The key doesn’t ask why you took it—it only knows you’ve seen what must be done.
