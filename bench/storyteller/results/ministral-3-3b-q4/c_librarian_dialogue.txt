The librarian exhales, as if holding breath for years. "Torn out before the flood," she repeats, fingers tracing the spine of a dusty tome. "The water took what was left, but this? *This* is older than the flood itself." She reaches for a yellowed ledger beside her, its ink smudged from long use. The name of the thief will not be in these pages—and if it’s been removed, it may never return.
