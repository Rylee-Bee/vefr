The key turns in the lock, but it does not give way. The chest is stubborn, its lock resistant to this key, this iron. You pull at it, feeling the resistance, the stubbornness. The chest does not yield.

The key slides into the lock, but it does not turn. You try again, and again. The lock holds fast, as if it does not recognize the key you have in your hand. The chest's secrets remain hidden, its contents untouched. Your frustration grows, but you know there is no force strong enough to make the lock give way.

---

The chest rattles on its feet, but the lock holds fast. It does not budge, even as your foot strikes it. The lid remains closed, and the contents untouched. Frustration fills the air, but it does not change the chest, locked as it was before you entered.
