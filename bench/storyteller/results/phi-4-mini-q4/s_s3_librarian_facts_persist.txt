The librarian looks up, startled. You ask about the missing page. She hesitates, brows furrowing as she recalls. "I don't know," she finally says. She shakes her head, as if your question is one of the books themselves - out of place, out of time. She cannot say who tore it out.

---

The librarian nods, the weight of her knowledge pressing down on her features. She confirms as much as she had the first time, as certain as the pages she guards. The flood came and tore the page out of the book, before it could be written by her hand. She knows, and she knows it well.
