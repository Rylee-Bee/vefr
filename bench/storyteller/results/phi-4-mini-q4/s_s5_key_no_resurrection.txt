A small iron key rests in the drawer's depths, nestled between sheets of parchment. It looks like it's been there for a while - an echo of a moment long past. You grasp it carefully, wondering what door this might open.

---

The iron key turns with a satisfying click, and the chest reveals its empty innards. No gold, no silver, nothing but the echo of your actions. The chest, at least, hasn't betrayed you. The key lies in your hand, a silent witness to the emptiness.

---

The drawer has always been bare, its contents as empty as the day they were used. Your fingers close around nothingness as you reach for the key again. It has already turned the lock, the door already creaks open. The drawer is empty, as always.
