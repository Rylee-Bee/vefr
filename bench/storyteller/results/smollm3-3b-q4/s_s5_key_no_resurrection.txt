The drawer is not what you expected - not what you thought you would find in the study. Instead of letters or maps, you find a small iron key. It is smooth and plain, but it might open something. It sits on the desk, waiting.

---

The key turns with a familiar click, and the chest groans open. Inside, there is nothing - no gold, no coin, no document, no sign of the treasure you sought. The key slips from your hand, cold and empty.

---

The drawer is empty, the key's imprint still faint on the lid. The desk is quiet now, except for the ticking of the clock. Whatever you missed, it's gone, or you've used it. The only thing left is the task ahead, and the key's promise of a way forward.
